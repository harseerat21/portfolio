The problem

People prep by reading questions, but struggle to tell a clear story out loud. Human mock interviews help, but they're costly and hard to book.

How might wegive anyone a realistic mock interview, any time, for free?

1. Summary

A website where MBA students find internships and jobs, book mentors, and apply in one step. Colleges and companies reach them through a simple form.

2. Problem

MBA students hunt for openings across LinkedIn, job boards and WhatsApp groups, and most of what they find isn't meant for them. Mentorship happens informally, if at all. The community had no home online, and no way to bring in the paying side.

3. Goals

  • Become the first place MBA students check for openings
  • Turn free visitors into mentorship bookings
  • Give companies a direct channel to MBA talent

Not in version 1

  • Paid courses and a community forum
  • A company portal (a form does the job)
  • Non-MBA aspirants
  • AI resume tools

Feature PRD: Internship and job search

Owner
Harseerat Kaur, PM
Version
1.0
Priority
Must, sprint 1
Parent
MVP platform PRD

1. Problem

Students waste time on openings that don't fit: wrong experience level, expired deadlines, unpaid roles they can't take. They need to narrow down fast and apply before the deadline.

2. User stories and acceptance criteria

As an MBA student, I want to filter openings by what matters to me, so that I only see roles I can apply to.

  • Given I'm on the internships page
  • When I choose Remote, Marketing and Paid only
  • Then only matching openings show, the count updates, and each filter appears as a removable chip

As an MBA student, I want to apply without retyping my details, so that applying takes about a minute.

  • Given my profile has a resume, email and phone
  • When I press Apply
  • Then a modal opens with those details filled in, shows any company questions, and submits in one step

As a student, I never want to see an opening that has closed, so that I don't waste an application.

  • Given an opening's deadline has passed
  • When I search or browse
  • Then it's hidden by default, and its detail page says it has closed

3. Requirements

IDRequirementPriority
S1Keyword search across role, company and skillsMust
S2Filters: experience, work mode, domain, duration (1 to 6 months), stipend, hide expiredMust
S3Cards show role, company, location, mode, duration, stipend and deadlineMust
S4Detail page: overview, eligibility, skills, perks, company, with save, share and applyMust
S5One-step apply modal with prefilled details and company questionsMust
S6Sort by deadline (default), newest or stipendShould
S7Save openings and get a reminder 48 hours before the deadlineShould
S8Similar openings and a matching mentor on the detail pageCould

4. Edge cases

  • Deadline passes mid-application: block submit and explain why
  • No resume on profile: upload inside the modal
  • Applying twice: show "Applied on 10 Feb" instead of the button
  • No results: suggest removing the last filter

5. Events to track

  • search_performed
  • filter_applied
  • opening_viewed
  • apply_started
  • apply_submitted
  • opening_saved

Feature PRD: Mentorship booking

Owner
Harseerat Kaur, PM
Version
1.0
Priority
Must, sprint 2
Parent
MVP platform PRD

1. Problem

Students want advice from someone who's been through the same placement or interview, but finding and scheduling that person is awkward. Alumni want to help, but only in slots that fit their week.

2. User stories and acceptance criteria

As an MBA student, I want to book a mentor for my goal, so that I prepare with someone who's done it.

  • Given I pick "Summer placements" and a domain
  • When I choose a mentor's open slot and confirm
  • Then I get an email with a calendar invite, and the slot disappears for others

As a student who doesn't know whom to pick, I want to be matched, so that choosing isn't a barrier.

  • Given I press "Match me with a mentor"
  • When I share my goal and domain
  • Then the team matches me by hand and I hear back within 24 hours

As a mentor, I want to set my own slots, so that mentoring fits around work.

  • Given I'm an approved mentor
  • When I share my weekly availability
  • Then only those slots are bookable, and I'm told about each booking

3. Requirements

IDRequirementPriority
M1Mentor list filtered by goal, domain, company and experienceMust
M2Mentor card: name, role, company, MBA year, rating, tags and next 3 slotsMust
M3Book a slot, with email confirmation and a calendar inviteMust
M4"Match me" request, matched by the team within 24 hoursMust
M5First session free for every studentShould
M6Rating and short review after each sessionCould
M7Paid sessions and automatic matchingWon't, for now

4. How it runs at launch

  • Mentors share availability through a Google Form
  • The team confirms each booking by email or WhatsApp
  • Mentor cancels: offer the student the next slot automatically
  • Student no-show: one free reschedule

5. Metrics

  • Time to match a "Match me" request
  • Bookings per mentor page visit
  • Sessions completed versus booked
  • Average rating and repeat bookings

A reason to sign up on day one.
